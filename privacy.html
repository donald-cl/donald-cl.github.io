Privacy policy

vibecoder · last updated 28 September 2026

1. What vibecoder is

vibecoder is a personal, single-user tool: a set of command-line scripts that their owner runs on their own device. It is not a public service and it has no users other than the owner. This policy describes what the tool does with the owner's own Google data.

2. Who the data controller is

The owner and sole operator of vibecoder is the individual reachable at donaldhui@gmail.com.

3. What data is accessed

When the owner grants consent, vibecoder accesses only the following data in the owner's own Google Account:

  • Google Drive, Docs and Sheets — only the files that vibecoder itself creates or that the owner explicitly opens with it. It does not browse the rest of the owner's Drive.
  • Gmail — read access to the owner's messages, and the ability to create drafts for the owner to review. Drafts are not sent without the owner's action.
  • Google Calendar — read access to the owner's calendars, and the ability to create events in them.

vibecoder does not request access to contacts, photos, location, payment information, or any other Google service.

4. Why it is accessed

Solely to carry out the task the owner asked for at that moment — for example writing a note into a document, summarising the owner's own mail, or adding an appointment to the owner's own calendar. There is no other purpose.

5. How the data is used, stored and shared

  • Data is processed on the owner's own device, at the moment of use.
  • Data is not sold, and is not shared or transferred to any other person, company or service.
  • Data is not used for advertising of any kind.
  • Data is not used to develop, improve or train generalised artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.
  • No analytics, tracking or third-party scripts are used by this website or by the tool.
  • There is no server: the tool talks to Google's APIs directly from the owner's device.

6. Tokens and credentials

The OAuth client credentials and the access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the owner's own device, in files whose permissions restrict them to the owner's account. They are never transmitted anywhere except to Google when requesting or refreshing access. Any local copies of fetched data live only on that device and are deleted by the owner at will.

7. Limited Use

vibecoder's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Retention and deletion

vibecoder keeps no database. Cached files and tokens on the device can be removed at any time by deleting the tool's local configuration directory, which immediately ends its access.

9. How to revoke access

Access granted to vibecoder can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions by selecting vibecoder and choosing Remove access. Revoking access invalidates the stored tokens immediately.

10. Children

vibecoder is a personal tool used by an adult and is not directed at children.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy: donaldhui@gmail.com.
