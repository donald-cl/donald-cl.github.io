vibecoder

A personal tool. Not a public service.

vibecoder is a small set of command-line scripts that its owner runs on their own phone. Its only job is to let those scripts read and write the owner's own Google data — Drive, Docs, Sheets, Gmail and Calendar — on the owner's behalf.

Who can use it

Only the owner. There is no sign-up, no hosted service, no web application and no second user. The Google OAuth client exists so that the owner's own scripts can ask the owner for permission to reach the owner's own account.

What it does

  • Creates and updates documents, spreadsheets and files in the owner's Google Drive.
  • Reads the owner's Gmail and prepares drafts for the owner to review and send.
  • Reads the owner's Google Calendar and creates events in it.

What it does not do

  • It does not serve other people, and it has no other users to serve.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone.
  • It does not use Google user data for advertising, and it does not use it to develop, improve or train generalised artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

Where the data lives

OAuth tokens and credentials stay on the owner's own device, in files readable only by the owner's account. Nothing is uploaded to a third-party server: the scripts talk to Google's APIs directly.
